## Introduction and Purpose
The purpose of this web-based system is allow registered members of DS to perform dice rolls of many different dice types easily. DS current roll their dice by hand to determine the outcome of actions in DnD. It is currently difficult for members to keep track of their dicerolls over many sessions and roll many dice simultaneously. This is a problem because Admins cannot verify that members really did perform a really difficult action several sessions ago, as well as perform large battles because many dice have to be rolled simultaneously.
